How Cityguy analyses matches

A hybrid statistical and contextual approach.

01 · Team strength

Recent form, home/away splits, goals for and against, squad availability and schedule congestion.

02 · Goal probabilities

xG, xGA and Poisson goal modelling to estimate scoreline and goals-market probabilities.

03 · Match context

Tactical matchup, injuries, suspensions and relevant situational factors, with uncertainty stated clearly.
